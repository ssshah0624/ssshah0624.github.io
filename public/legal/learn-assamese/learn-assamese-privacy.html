Learn Assamese

Privacy Policy

Effective July 13, 2026

Learn Assamese is designed to work without a Brightwood account and without collecting personal data through the app.

1. Information collection

Brightwood does not collect personal information, usage data, or advertising identifiers through Learn Assamese. Your lesson progress, review history, streaks, and preferences are maintained by the app on your device.

2. Audio and learning content

The app plays included Assamese pronunciation audio. Listening to lessons and completing exercises does not send your answers or learning history to Brightwood.

3. Purchases and device services

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions, so Brightwood does not receive your full payment-card details. Notifications, device backups, and purchase management are controlled by your Apple settings and governed by Apple’s policies.

4. Support messages

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it only to answer your request and may retain it as needed for support and ordinary business records.

5. Sharing, retention, and choices

Because Brightwood does not collect app data, we do not sell or share it. You can reset locally stored information by deleting the app. A device backup may retain app data according to your Apple backup settings.

6. Children’s privacy

Learn Assamese is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Contact admin@brightwoodapps.com with questions.
